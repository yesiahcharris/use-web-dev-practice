The Three Branches of Government

  1. Legislative Branch
  2. Executive Branch
  3. Judicial Branch

The three branches of government are the legislative, executive, and judicial branches.The legislative branch makes the laws, the executive branch enforces the laws, and the judicial branch interprets the laws.

What is a Democracy?

A democracy is a system of government where the people have the authority to make decisions pertaining to the government, typically through an election-based process with representatives

Also, democracies are established to ensure the government is accountable to the people and does not abuse their power.
